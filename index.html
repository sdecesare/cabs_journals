About this explorer

This is a simple browser for the supplied Academic Journal Guide / ABS journal list. Ratings are displayed on the familiar AJG scale, where the source value 5 is shown as 4*. Historical ratings and external profile links are included when available.

Independent search interface; not affiliated with or endorsed by the Chartered Association of Business Schools. Verify important publication decisions against the official source.
